## Task list for agents

### P0

**N1. The phone clock can still farm rewards, and the game can get stuck weeks in the future**
- Evidence (clock moved by the probe, reloading each time):

  | Phone date | Game day | Free gift | Practice stars left | Quests |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | today (2 Nov) | 2 Nov | opened | 150 | 2 Nov |
  | +1 day | 3 Nov | **new gift opened** | **150 again** | new |
  | +2 days | 4 Nov | **new gift opened** | **150 again** | new |
  | +3 days | 5 Nov | **new gift opened** | **150 again** | new |
  | +30 days | **2 Dec** | ready | 150 | 2 Dec |
  | put back to today | **still 2 Dec** | — | — | **2 Dec** |
  | real tomorrow | **still 2 Dec** | — | — | **no new quests** |

  1. Every forward change of the date gives a fresh gift, a fresh 150-star practice allowance and new quests, with no limit.
  2. After one big jump forward and back, the game day stays in the future. The child gets **no new quests, gift or streak day for a month**. Because "the later day wins" on a cloud load, that stuck date also copies to their other devices.
- Where: `js/core.js` `WR.today()` (~line 97): `if (cal > c.day) c.day = cal;`, plus the cloud merge of `state.clock`.
- Fix: use a trusted date when online. Every GitHub Pages response has a `Date` header, so `fetch('index.html', { method: 'HEAD', cache: 'no-store' })` gives the server's date with no new backend.
  - **Online:** the game day = the server date (Singapore time, or the device's time zone applied to the server UTC time). If the saved game day is ahead of the server date, bring it back down to the server date. Keep stars and progress; only the "day" moves back.
  - **Offline:** allow the device date, but move at most **one** game day per app session. When the app is next online, line it up with the server date again.
  - In the cloud merge, take the later of the two days **only if it isn't later than the server date**.
- Done when, re-running the `R22 clock` probe in `tests-2026-11-02/loop3.js`:
  - online, a +1/+2/+3 day jump gives **no** new gift, allowance or quests;
  - after a +30 day jump and back, the next real day gives new quests as normal;
  - offline, two jumps in one session give at most one new day.

### P1

**N2. Daily quests don't match the child**
- Evidence: a brand-new player who chose A1 English and never opened Maths gets "Balance an equation scale all the way to x (Grades 6–8 maths)" and "Solve 3 maths questions with a Model Magic picture" (`shots-2026-11-02/se_quests_grade6-8_for_A1.png`).
- Where: the quest pool in `js/core.js` / `js/main.js` (`WR.screens.quests`, the daily quest roll).
- Fix: only roll quests the child can do now. English quests must come from unlocked realms and open games (no Writing quest while Writing is locked). Maths quests only after a maths grade is chosen or placed, and only for topics in that grade. At most 1 maths quest a day for a child who has played English only.
- Done when: a new A1 player sees only A1-doable quests for 7 rolled days in a row (test by moving the game day in a test).

**N3. The boss screen at 320 px pushes the pet card off the edge**
- Evidence: in the B2 boss on 320×658, the boss card and the pet card share a row, and the pet card is squeezed to a sliver with half the pet hidden (`320_boss_pet_card_cut.png`).
- Fix: under 360 px in boss mode, hide the pet card (keep the boss, its hearts and the ⭐/🔥 chips), or stack the pet under the boss card as a single 40 px row.
- Done when: nothing is cut at the right edge on the boss screen at 320 and 344 px.

**N4. The home info pills are cut off at the right edge**
- Evidence: under "Hello, Mia!", the row "✅ 0 correct answers · 📕 Storybook 0…" runs past the card edge with no scroll cue ("Storybook 0" at 375 px, "Sto" at 320 px; `320_home_pills_clipped.png`, `se_home.png`).
- Fix: let the pills wrap onto a second line (`flex-wrap: wrap`), or show at most 2 short pills on phones.
- Done when: every pill is fully visible at 320–430 px.

**N5. The 🔊 button squeezes the sentence in the new word games**
- Evidence: in Word Bridges, the punctuation game, Spell Swap and Word Forge, the round 56 px sound button sits inside the sentence box. The sentence wraps around it with big gaps ("I have ____ / little cat.", `se_cloze_sound_button_crowds_text.png`, `320_forge.png`).
- Fix: move 🔊 into the card header next to the title ("A1 · My Cat Coco 🔊"), or make it a small 40 px button under the sentence, so the sentence uses the full width.
- Done when: in those four games at 320 and 375 px, each sentence line runs the full card width.

**N6. The cloud chip in the top bar now looks heavier than its neighbours**
- Evidence: the ☁️ chip has a thick lilac ring and looks bigger than the ⭐ and 🔥 chips next to it.
- Fix: the same height as the other chips, with a thin outline (about 1.5 px). Keep 3:1 contrast with a darker cloud icon instead of the thick ring.

**N7. Too many bonus games per realm**
- Evidence: with everything unlocked, A1 has **10 bonus games** next to the 4 main skills: Petal Match, Speaking, Writing, Story, Phonics, Word Charms, Spell Swap, Word Forge, Word Bridges and the punctuation game. One-by-one unlocking helps new players, but the row keeps growing with every new feature.
- Fix: stop adding new top-level tiles. Group the bonus games into 3 doors: **🎤 Say it** (Speaking, Phonics), **✍️ Write it** (Writing, Spell Swap, Word Forge, Word Bridges, Punctuation) and **🎲 Play** (Petal Match, Word Charms, Story). Future question types should go inside the main lessons or into a group, not into new tiles.
- Done when: at most 4 main skills + 3 bonus doors + the boss are shown in a realm.
