# Word Realms — phone review (30 Oct 2026)

Live site: https://jere8899-collab.github.io/word-realms/ · Repo: `jere8899-collab/word-realms` (commit `f4cfd1c`)

## How this was tested
- Played the live site in Chrome with phone emulation (touch, mobile UA, real pixel density) on 7 profiles:
  iPhone SE 375×667 · iPhone 15 393×852 · iPhone 15 Pro Max 430×932 · Galaxy A 360×780 · narrow phone / Fold cover 320×658 · phone landscape 844×390 · iPad mini 768×1024.
- On every profile: onboarding → map → A1 realm → full grammar lesson (one wrong answer, rest right) → result → listening, reading, speaking, writing, Petal Match → B2 boss → C2 reading → Maths → Pet → Shop → Garden.
- Also: slow-4G and 3G cold loads with a 4× slower CPU, frame-rate check with a 4× slower CPU, tap-target sizes, sideways overflow, and a read of the code and question bank.
- Scripts: `review.js`, `slow.js`, `wide.js` in this folder. Screenshots: `shots/`.
- **Limit:** this was Chrome emulating phones, not real Safari on a real iPhone. Items marked **[verify on device]** need a real iPhone or Android check.

## What is already good (keep it)
- **Zero console errors** on all 7 profiles.
- **Fast:** about 285 KB first load. The name screen appears in about 2.4 s on slow 4G and 4.5 s on 3G. The map is ready about 1.2 s after onboarding.
- **Smooth:** about 57 fps on the map, still and while scrolling, even with a 4× slower CPU.
- The art is lovely and consistent. Answer feedback (red/green, the pet reacting) is clear. Fresh-first question picking, the Mistake Garden, and the shadowing fallback for speaking are strong features.

## Task list for agents

Each task has: **Problem** (with evidence) → **Where** → **Fix** → **Done when**. The order is the order to do them in.

### P0 — fix first (breaks the experience on common phones)

**T1. The page is wider than the screen on phones under 352 px**
- Problem: on 320 px and 344 px screens (Galaxy Z Fold cover, older and small Androids, iPhone SE 1st gen), the browser's real width becomes 352 px. The page zooms out or slides sideways, and the "Garden" tab is cut off (`shots/fold_narrow_06_lesson_wrong.png`).
- Where: `css/style.css`. `.navbar` (5 buttons about 62 px each, plus gaps) and the top bar `.chips` row (stars, streak, cloud, settings, plus the brand) can't shrink.
- Fix: navbar buttons get `flex:1 1 0; min-width:0`. Under 360 px, use smaller labels or icons only. In the top bar under 380 px, hide the "Word Realms" text and keep the avatar. Let `.chips` shrink (`min-width:0`, smaller gap).
- Done when: at 320 and 344 px, `innerWidth === 320/344` on the home, lesson, shop and maths screens, and all 5 tabs are fully visible.

**T2. On small phones, the answers sit below the fold during lessons**
- Problem: answers fully visible without scrolling when a question loads: iPhone SE **1 of 4**, Galaxy A **3 of 4**, 320 px **0 of 4**, landscape **0 of 4** (iPhone 15 and up: 4 of 4). The question starts about 460 px down. Above it sit the "Leave" row, a tall progress card (8 sprouts in a 2×4 block) and the pet-bubble card (`shots/iphoneSE_05_lesson_q.png`). In the **B2 boss timed mode** this means the timer runs while the child is still scrolling (`shots/iphoneSE_14_b2_boss.png`).
- Where: `js/lesson.js` (lesson header markup) and `css/style.css` (`@media (max-width:760px)` lesson rules).
- Fix:
  1. Hide the bottom navbar during lessons, specials, speaking, writing and the placement quiz. They already have "← Leave", and the navbar takes about 85 px.
  2. Merge the progress card and the pet card into one slim row (about 64 px): sprouts in a single row with smaller icons, a small pet with a short bubble, and stars/combo as small chips.
  3. Don't start the boss timer until the question and options are on screen (or start it after the first scroll or tap).
- Done when: on 375×667 and 360×780, the question and all 4 options are visible without scrolling for grammar, vocab and boss questions. Listening and reading can scroll, but their play button and first option must be visible.

**T3. Confetti spills off-screen and covers content**
- Problem: `WR.confetti` (`js/core.js` ~line 144) places pieces at `left: 0–100vw`, so pieces stick out past the right edge and widen the page during every celebration. Confetti also falls over the next screen's text for about 3 s. In `shots/se_speaking.png` it covers the sentence the child has to read. There is no `prefers-reduced-motion` handling (only `math.css` has it; `style.css` has 19 infinite animations).
- Fix: put the confetti in one `position:fixed; inset:0; overflow:hidden; pointer-events:none` layer and use `left` up to about 92vw. Fire it only on result screens and big rewards, not when a screen opens. Clear it on `WR.go()`. Add a global `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` that turns off confetti, sparkles and the infinite animations.
- Done when: no sideways overflow during or after confetti, nothing falls over question or speaking text, and reduced-motion users see no confetti.

**T4. The welcome toast covers screen titles**
- Problem: "Puffbun is so happy to meet you!" stays on screen into the next screen and covers the title (`shots/se_speaking.png`, `shots/iphoneSE_03_home.png`, where it also hides the ✏️ rename button).
- Fix: on phones, show toasts at the bottom above the navbar (or as a small top-center pill under the header, not over content). Auto-hide after 2.5 s and clear on `WR.go()`.
- Done when: no toast is visible after you move to another screen, and none covers a button.

### P2 — polish and long-term smoothness

## Content — what to add or improve

**C1. The question bank is thin for a whole CEFR level (biggest content gap).**
About 30 items per skill per level and 8 questions per lesson means only about 4 fresh lessons per skill, about 16 lessons per realm, or roughly 1–2 hours of play per CEFR level. A real CEFR level takes about 100–200 hours of guided learning. Goal: at least **120 items per skill per level** (4×), starting with **A1–B1** (the most likely audience for this art style). Keep the existing build checks (`tools/build_data.py`: fair-distractor rule, duplicate check).

**C2. Too many multiple-choice questions.** Measured: grammar 170 MCQ + 12 sentence-order items (A2 only); vocab 183 MCQ; listening 168 single-sentence MCQ. Add question types, each reusable at every level:
- type-the-word / spelling (with a letter-tile keyboard for A1–A2)
- sentence builder (tiles) at every level, not only A2
- find-the-mistake (tap the wrong word)
- gap-fill from a word bank (2–3 gaps)
- **picture choice for A1** ("Picture Pop" grammar currently has no pictures)
- listen-and-order, dictation (type what you hear), and short 2–4 line dialogues for listening

**C3. Explanations are too short to teach.** The median explanation is **36 characters**, and 325 of 541 are under 40. Example: "There ___ a dragon" → "One thing: 'there is'." Write each one as **rule + why the picked answer is wrong + one example**, for example: "We say *there is* for one thing (a dragon). *am* only goes with *I* — *I am happy.*" Show the wrong-answer note tailored to the option the child picked (store a short note per distractor).

**C4. Listening tests word-matching, not understanding.** Many items say the answer word for word ("The cat is under the table." → "under the table"). From A2 up, paraphrase the right option, add numbers, times and changes of mind ("at 3… no, at 4"), and use 2-speaker dialogues.

**C5. Audience — DECIDED by Jeremy (30 Oct 2026): the game is for children, and the cute art stays everywhere.** Do not make a "grown-up" skin for C1/C2 or high-school maths. Instead, when writing or rewriting C1/C2 (and Grade 9–12 maths word problems), choose topics that bright older children and young teens care about: animals, space, inventions, school life, games, friendship and fairytale worlds. Avoid adult topics (workplace, politics, finance, relationships). Keep the grammar and vocabulary at the real CEFR level; only the topics change. Example: keep the C2 inversion/nuance items, but set the passage in the Starlight Library with a young apprentice instead of a dead librarian's estate.

**C6. Things to aim for between lessons.** Stars only buy a few cosmetic items. Add sticker-book or collection pages per realm, pet evolutions shown as goals on the map ("Lv 5 → aura"), weekly events, and a "realm boss" unlock at the end of each realm.

**C7. Payments for children — DECIDED by Jeremy (30 Oct 2026): add a parent check.** See task **T19** below. Before payments go live, also check Apple/Google rules for children's apps and privacy rules (PDPA/COPPA) if accounts or cloud saves collect data from children.

**T19. Parent check (P1 — do before any real payment is wired up)**
- Problem: "💳 Buy with cash" buttons in the Shop (`js/pet-shop.js`, which calls the `PaymentHook` stub in `js/payments.js`) can be tapped straight away by a child.
- Fix:
  1. Add `WR.parentGate(onPass)` (new small file `js/parent-gate.js`, loaded before `pet-shop.js`). It opens a modal: "Grown-ups only 🔒". It asks a question a young child can't easily answer but an adult can, chosen at random. Example: "Type the number *forty-seven*" (written in words, answered in digits), or a 2-digit × 1-digit sum written in words. Show a number pad, 3 tries, then a 60-second lock-out.
  2. Every cash action goes through the gate first: "Buy with cash", the `demoMode` unlock, and any future subscription or "remove ads" button. Cloud Castle "Log out & clear device" and Teacher mode in ⚙️ Settings should also be gated.
  3. After a pass, the gate stays open for 5 minutes only (kept in memory, never saved), then asks again.
  4. Move cash items behind a "👪 Grown-ups" area in the Shop. Children see premium items with a "Ask a grown-up 💖" label instead of a price button. Stars-only items stay as they are.
  5. Add an optional 4-digit parent PIN in ⚙️ Settings → Grown-ups. If a PIN is set, the gate asks for it instead of the question. Save it hashed (SHA-256 via `crypto.subtle`) in the save file so it syncs with cloud saves.
  6. Child-friendly copy: no "payment", "card" or price wording in front of the gate; prices appear only after the gate.
- Test: new `tools/test/parentgate.js`. Check: a cash button opens the gate; a wrong answer ×3 locks for 60 s; the right answer reaches the existing `PaymentHook` stub; the gate re-asks after 5 min (fake the clock); the PIN flow works; the gate fits on 375×667 and 320×658 with the number pad visible; no console errors.
- Done when: no cash, demo-unlock, clear-device or Teacher-mode action can run without passing the gate.
